<h2>v1.0.10 - Tower placement fix</h2>

<h3>Fixed</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Towers spawning underwater or on mountain slopes</strong>: the three simple waystone tower families (normal, ruins, pillager) were using <code>project_start_to_heightmap: OCEAN_FLOOR_WG</code>, which placed them relative to the ocean floor even on land, causing them to appear buried, floating, or embedded in terrain. Changed to <code>WORLD_SURFACE_WG</code> so they sit on the actual top solid block, preventing underwater/mountain placements.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<blockquote>Purely world-gen change. Does not affect behavior of player-placed waystones or light beams.</blockquote>
